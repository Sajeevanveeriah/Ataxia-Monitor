# Fabrication revision boundary

`20261021-VW-MTC1-BOM-Rev02.csv` describes the current Rev B KiCad design.
It contains 160 fitted components and one DNP component, grouped by part and fit
status. Test points and mechanical items marked as excluded from the BOM remain
in KiCad.

The dated Rev00 Gerber/drill ZIP, pick-and-place CSV and STEP model are retained
as historical **Rev A** exports. The Rev00 and Rev01 BOMs also describe Rev A.
Do not mix those exports with the Rev02 BOM.

Regenerate and review a matching fabrication set from the final Rev B source
before ordering. Review the outstanding CAD warnings and complete the bench
checks in [the Rev01 touch design note](../../../docs/20261021-Touch-Redesign-Rev01.md).
